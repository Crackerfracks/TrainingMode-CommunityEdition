# Visibility

Visibility settings make cues, paths and timers fainter or bolder, and fade them out as you learn them.

Auto Fade dims each cue as you start hitting it. Get consistent enough and the event quietly leaves the room.

## Options

Intensity is in the Cues menu. The other rows are in Cues, then Intensity by Group.

| Option | Values | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Intensity | 1 Faint, 2 Soft, 3 Standard, 4 Bold, 5 Boldest | 3 Standard | How strongly the cues, paths and timers show, from faint to bold. Standard is the usual look. Set each group apart from it in Intensity by Group. |
| Cues | 2 Fainter, 1 Fainter, Same, 1 Bolder, 2 Bolder | Same | Body Flash and Platform Glow, against the Intensity level. |
| Paths | 2 Fainter, 1 Fainter, Same, 1 Bolder, 2 Bolder | Same | The landing and body paths, frame dots, input markers and slide-off line, against the Intensity level. |
| Timers | 2 Fainter, 1 Fainter, Same, 1 Bolder, 2 Bolder | Same | Every timer: the strip, near Falcon, the landing spot, waveland and wavedash, against the Intensity level. |
| Ledge | 2 Fainter, 1 Fainter, Same, 1 Bolder, 2 Bolder | Same | Ledge routes, their inputs and their timers, against the Intensity level. |
| Controller | 1 Faint, 2 Soft, 3 Standard, 4 Bold, 5 Boldest | 3 Standard | The controller display, on its own level. The main Intensity does not change it. |
| Auto Fade | Off, Timers, Cues, Both | Off | Fades the timers or cues as you learn a timing. Each kind (AI, NIL, waveland) follows how many of your last 10 or so tries hit. A miss brings it back a little, and a run of misses brings it all the way back. |
| Reset Fade | Press A | n/a | Forgets the hit rates Auto Fade goes by, so everything shows in full again. |

## How to read it

- Intensity is one dial for everything. The group rows move one group up or down from that level. With Intensity on 3 Standard, Timers on 1 Fainter puts the timers one step below Standard and leaves the rest alone.
- The Controller is the exception. It has its own level, and Intensity does not change it.
- The group rows use the Intensity level as their starting point, so changing Intensity moves every group except the controller.

## Auto Fade

Auto Fade keeps a hit rate for each kind of cue (AI, NIL, waveland) from your last 10 or so tries. The higher the rate, the fainter that kind gets. A miss brings it back a little. A run of misses brings it back to full.

- Timers fades the timers.
- Cues fades the cues.
- Both fades both.

Use Reset Fade when you change what you are practicing and want everything at full strength again.

## Tips

- Put Intensity on 1 Faint or 2 Soft once you know where a cue is, instead of turning cues off.
- Use the group rows when one thing is too loud. Paths on 1 Fainter keeps the timers as they were.
- Auto Fade is also a row in the quick menu, so you can change it between attempts. See [Presets](presets.md).
