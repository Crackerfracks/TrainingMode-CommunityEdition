# His nair, bair and uair are his own states (347-349, landing in 350-352),

# nair 15 frames in 350, bair 18 in 351, uair 18 in 352 (his bair's lag,

# ("Lag", never "L-cancel"). Fair and dair are the common ones: with L
# their lag halves (12 in LandingAirF, 10 in LandingAirLw) and the line
# says "L-cancel". Landing in an aerial's no-lag frames is "Land" (Landing).
