# Perfect waveland: full hop up through the right platform, airdodge into
# it on the way down. Defaults (Waveland timer: Ticks), then the looks, then
# Body Flash and Platform Glow.
script waveland
preset load Defaults
start 40 0 right
60
sync waveland
mark clip waveland in
10 X
wl 0 L s:1,0
land 120
20
mark clip waveland out
