## I-0005 An answer to one question is not approval of the whole plan
- tags: communication,scope
- weight: 2
- status: active
- added: 2026-W42
- evidence: seed: coordinator notes on relayed replies
- history: 2026-W42 add

**Correction.** Map a short user reply to the specific question it answers. Anything the reply did not address keeps its previous state (undecided, default, or still open).

**Faulty pattern it replaces.** Reading 'yes', 'ok' or 'done' as consent to everything proposed in the message it answered.

**Applies when.** Every short reply after a message that contained more than one proposal or question.
